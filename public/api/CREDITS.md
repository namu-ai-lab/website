# /api page film

The film behind the `/api` hero (`storyteller.webm`, `storyteller.mp4`, poster `storyteller.jpg`; set in `FILM` in
`src/content/api.ts`) is licensed stock footage. It plays muted, as decoration. The page does not say who the people
are, and never presents them as Namu users or as people speaking through the Lab's models.

| Files | Source | Filmmaker | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `storyteller.*` | [Traditional Ekpeye storytelling gathering outdoors](https://www.pexels.com/video/traditional-ekpeye-storytelling-gathering-outdoors-31560636/) | Armstrong Opulency | [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/) |

Re-encoded for the web (1280 px wide, 25 fps, no audio: H.264 and VP9), nothing else changed.

The footage was filmed among the Ekpeye people of southern Nigeria, not in the Sahel. Replace it with footage from the
Sahel, ideally filmed by Namu with releases, before the page leans on it as a picture of the region.
