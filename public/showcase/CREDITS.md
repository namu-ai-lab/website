# Showcase clips

Create's showcases (`src/content/ideas.ts`, `SHOWCASES`) are short cuts of freely licensed footage from Wikimedia
Commons, filmed by people from Niger, Hausaland and northern Ghana for Wiki Loves Africa, Wikitongues and Commons'
own campaigns. The people shown are examples,
not Namu users. Each cut is shared under the same licence as its source (CC BY-SA requires it).

What Namu added: for every clip but the storyteller, a Hausa line said by Tafiya in Nadia's voice through the Namu API
on 2026-11-01 (two takes each; the one Tatsuniya transcribed closest to the script was kept), laid over the footage with
its own sound kept low. For the storyteller and the vox pop, nothing was added: Tatsuniya wrote down the speakers' own
voices and Kora put them in French; both are shown unedited. Clips showing children, partners' logos or a watermark were
left out.
